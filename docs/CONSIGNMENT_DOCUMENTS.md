# Consignment documents — electronics and fabric

First cut of [#27](https://github.com/nakultt/iqoo/issues/27): which papers an
electronics or fabric consignment needs, which of them the **law** requires and
which only **commerce** expects, and the clocks they start. Scope is central
law as it applies in Tamil Nadu, seen from the receiving side.

The same table is encoded as data in
[`app/.../data/documents/`](../app/src/main/java/com/veritransit/inspector/data/documents/)
(`DocumentRegistry.ALL`). Nothing on this page is legal advice. In line with
the README's non-goal, the app does not verify these documents, rule on
legality, file returns, or speak for GSTN.

## Legally required

| Document | When | Who owes it | Rule | Mark |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tax invoice travels with the goods | Every supply | Person in charge of the conveyance | r.138A(1)(a); copy marked DUPLICATE FOR TRANSPORTER, r.48(1) | Primary (#27); r.48(1) Primary |
| Delivery challan issued, and travels | Job work, or any movement that is not a supply | Principal / consigner | r.45(1)–(2), r.55(1)–(2); r.138A(1)(a) | Primary; r.138A Primary (#27) |
| E-way bill generated | Consignment value **exceeds** ₹50,000 | Registered person causing the movement | r.138(1) | Primary |
| E-way bill generated by the **recipient** | Unregistered supplier, registered recipient known at the start | Recipient | r.138(3) Explanation 1 | Primary |
| E-way bill generated at **any** value | Job work sent to another State | Principal, or the job worker if registered | r.138(1) proviso | Primary |
| E-way bill travels | Whenever generated and moving by road — not rail, air or vessel. No road validity until Part B is filled | Person in charge of the conveyance | r.138A(1)(b); r.138(3) Explanation 2 | Primary (#27); Primary |
| E-invoice with IRN | Supplier notified for e-invoicing; issued any other way it "shall not be treated as an invoice" | Supplier | r.48(4)–(5) | Primary — threshold is gap G7 |
| Lots: complete invoice before the first lot, a challan per later lot, a certified invoice copy with each, the original with the last | Goods sent SKD/CKD or in batches | Supplier | r.55(5) | Primary |
| Bill of entry travels | Imported goods | Person in charge of the conveyance | r.138A(1) second proviso | Primary (#27) |
| FORM GST ITC-04 | Job work | Principal | r.45(3) | Primary |
| Accounts and records kept | Always | Every registered party | CGST Act s.36 | Primary (#27) |
| Lorry receipt (goods receipt) | Road, common carrier | Common carrier | Carriage by Road Act 2007 s.9 | **Secondary** — gaps G1, G2 |

**Consignment value** (r.138(1) Explanation 2) is the section 15 value on the
invoice, bill of supply or challan, **plus** the taxes and cess charged in it,
**minus** exempt supply billed on the same invoice. It is neither the grand
total nor the taxable value alone, so it is computed, never copied.

## Clocks

| Clock | Runs | Rule | Mark |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| E-way bill validity | 1 day up to 200 km, +1 day per further 200 km **or part** (20 km for over-dimensional cargo). A day ends at the midnight after the generation date | r.138(10) and Explanation 1 | Primary |
| Validity extension | Within 8 hours of expiry | r.138(10) proviso | Primary |
| Inputs back from the job worker | 1 year from sending, else deemed supplied that day | CGST Act s.143(3); r.45(4) | Primary (#27) |
| Capital goods back from the job worker | 3 years | CGST Act s.143(4) | Primary (#27) |
| ITC-04 | 25th of the month after the half-year (preceding-year turnover above ₹5 crore) or the financial year | r.45(3) and Explanation | Primary |
| Credit note for short or damaged goods | Declared by 30 November after the financial year of supply, or the annual return date if earlier | CGST Act s.34(2) | Primary (#27) |
| Record retention | 72 months from the annual return's due date | CGST Act s.36 | Primary (#27) |
| Written notice to a road carrier | 180 days from **booking**, before any suit | Carriage by Road Act 2007 s.16 | **Secondary** |

## Commercially customary — not law

| Document | Why it exists anyway |
| --- | --- |
| Purchase order | The anchor of the PO ↔ invoice ↔ goods-received-note match |
| Packing list | What was packed, carton by carton; what the count reconciles against |
| Goods received note | The receiver's declaration. Sign "subject to inspection" until counted — never a clean receipt for unchecked goods (#27 Part 3) |
| Credit note | No statute makes the supplier issue one; s.34(2) only limits how late it can be declared |
| Cargo insurance certificate, survey report | Needed to claim, not to move (#27 Part 3) |

## Not required — do not flag

- **Retail-pack declarations** (maker, net quantity, MRP …) on master cartons,
  rolls or bales. Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules 2011 r.6 attaches
  them to packages for retail sale. *Unverified here* — #27 records the
  correction but not its source.

## Electronics

- High value in few cartons: a single carton can cross ₹50,000.
- Sets and machines sent SKD/CKD or in lots follow r.55(5). The original
  invoice arrives only with the **last** lot.
- Imported components and devices travel with the bill of entry.
- "Hearing aids" were in the r.138(14) Annexure as first notified, but not in
  the one in force. Do not treat them as e-way-bill-free until r.138(14)(e) is
  checked.

## Fabric

- Job work (weaving, dyeing, printing, processing) is routine. It means a
  challan out, ITC-04, a one-year return clock, and an e-way bill at **any**
  value when the job worker is in another State.
- Buying from an unregistered weaver makes the **registered buyer** the one
  causing the movement, so the buyer owes the e-way bill.
- The Annexure as first notified listed "Khadi yarn", never khadi fabric, and
  the one in force lists neither.

## Corrections to #27 found while encoding

1. **Triplicate copies are r.48(1)**, not r.46: ORIGINAL FOR RECIPIENT /
   DUPLICATE FOR TRANSPORTER / TRIPLICATE FOR SUPPLIER (CBIC). r.48(6) lifts
   this for an e-invoice.
2. **The Annexure in force has 8 entries** (LPG, PDS kerosene, postal baggage,
   precious stones and metals, jewellery other than imitation, currency, used
   personal effects, coral), per CBIC. The long list #27 describes — khadi,
   hearing aids, spacecraft — matches the Annexure as first notified (per a
   reproduction). Nil-rated goods now leave the e-way bill through
   **r.138(14)(e)**, which points at the Schedule to Notification
   2/2017-Central Tax (Rate). That Schedule has not been read. So "mainstream
   electronics and fabric always need an e-way bill above ₹50,000" holds
   against the Annexure, but it is not yet checked against (e).

## QR codes carry headers only

- An **e-invoice QR** carries supplier and recipient GSTIN, document number,
  type and date, total invoice value, the **count** of line items, the main
  HSN, the IRN and its date. It never carries the lines themselves (NIC
  verification manual and GST portal FAQ, per #27 Part 2).
- The app's carton-label scan (`QrLabel`) extracts only a PO and a
  packing-list reference. Packed lines always come from a document read or
  manual entry, and are confirmed on the Packing List step.
- Whether an **e-way bill QR** carries Part A is still open (G3).
